IRCL: Cosmic Chaos

Get ready to glow—and throw down! IRCL is bringing Cosmic Chaos to ABU Games Battleground Gym in Boise on Saturday, October 17, 2026. Blacklight arenas, UV-painted floors, and two rings running all day.

Event Details

When & Where

Saturday, October 17, 2026, 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM MDT at ABU Games Battleground Gym, 7211 W Colonial St, Boise, ID 83709.

Get Directions

Weight Classes

1 lb Plastic Antweight (PLAnt), 1 lb Antweight, and 3 lb Beetleweight. Every class gets an additional 50 g weight allowance for UV paint, LEDs, and other glowing galactic pizazz.

Two Arenas, One Blacklight

Plastic Antweight fights in the 4×4×2 ft arena while Antweight and Beetleweight run in the 8×8×4 ft arena. Both are lit by blacklight over UV-painted floors, so build to glow.

Registration

Registration is open August 23 through October 16, 2026 on Robot Combat Events. Sign up early—brackets fill and registration closes the day before fights.
